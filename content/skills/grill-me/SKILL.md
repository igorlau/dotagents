Use the `grilling` skill to interview the user about the plan or design they describe.

## Repository conventions first

Before you start, read what the repository defines: agent instructions (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.github/copilot-instructions.md`, `.github/instructions/`, `.cursor/rules/`), project skills, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, style guides, linter and formatter configuration, and the patterns in the surrounding code. They take precedence over this skill: where they differ, follow the repository, and point out the difference instead of changing the repository's standard. The interview grounds its recommendations in those conventions.
